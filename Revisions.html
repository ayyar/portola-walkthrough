Portola / The common rooms

Room for stillness.
Room for living.

Four distinct palettes, twelve ways to inhabit the house. A selective refresh of the existing rooms: three garden-door treatments, a mirror, television or artwork above the fireplace, and a quieter balance between the two corridor walls.

Walk through with textured finishes, change the light and season, or sit at the sofa to see the garden at eye level. Each film follows one continuous two-minute route, accompanied by an original instrumental score for its theme.
