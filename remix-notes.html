A few considered additions.

The existing furniture, four palettes and twelve combinations remain. Each theme now compares the following details:

  1. Timber lift-and-slide glazing with satin hardware, paired with a compact organic walnut-framed mirror. Two panes stack alongside a fixed pane.
  2. A pocket garden door with a shallow enclosure beside the existing opening, paired with a framed television above the mantel.
  3. Four outward-folding glass leaves, paired with a quiet mineral relief above the mantel. The folding leaves use terrace space.

Facing the garden, the left corridor wall holds the arrival seat, a smaller luminous kumiko and its key ledge. A single textile appears farther along, beyond the kitchen opening and arch. The right wall stays clear around bedroom doors, with two short concealed ceiling washes. Look back along the hall as well as forward.

The fixed window at the end of the corridor remains a window. The garden doors use the separate great-room opening.

The mirror is an approximate custom study inspired by Porada Giolino, using its 100 × 76 × 3 cm envelope. Hardware draws on the supplied photograph. Door geometry illustrates mechanisms, not manufacturer CAD: pocket framing and drainage reference and wood folding reference.

Final door installation needs measured framing, drainage and threshold detailing. The television mounting and fireplace heat clearance need the selected products checked together. All dimensions remain illustrative.

Films: twelve continuous two-minute routes with the existing four instrumental scores. Published gallery with the selectively refreshed rooms and films.
